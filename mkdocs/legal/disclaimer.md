    The pages on opengraphtester.com are general information. They are not legal advice, and they are not a professional recommendation about how a page should be shared. The cards show how this tool read the tags on that visit. They are

    ### The Preview

    Preview reads the Open Graph and Twitter Card tags on a public page you name. It is not a complete list of everything an app might use, and it is not a check that the page is safe to open. A missing image means this tool did not receive
    an image it could show. It does not mean the page has no image. A card that differs from the app you later open is not proof that the app is wrong.
